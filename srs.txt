SOFTWARE REQUIREMENTS SPECIFICATION
Digital Conference Platform

3rd National Agroecology Symposium

1. Introduction
1.1 Purpose
This Software Requirements Specification (SRS) defines the complete functional and non-functional requirements for the official digital platform of the 3rd National Agroecology Symposium (NAS 2026). The document is intended for use by the development team, Symposium organizers, and stakeholders involved in the design, implementation, testing, and deployment of the platform.
The platform must provide a world-class, end-to-end digital experience  from pre-event registration and payment processing, through live-event engagement, to post-event archiving comparable to international benchmarks such as the African Food Systems Forum (AGRF), the International Biomass Conference & Expo (IBCE), and the CGIAR Food Systems Innovation Summit platforms.
1.2 Project Overview
The 3rd National Agroecology Symposium is a flagship two-day event bringing together over 300 participants including policymakers, researchers, farmers, civil society, private sector, development partners, and media across Rwanda and the region. Attendance is paid, and the platform must support a full commercial ticketing and payment workflow. Exhibitors (20+) spanning Platinum, Gold, and Silver tiers require dedicated management capabilities.
1.3 Scope
The platform encompasses:
A public-facing symposium website with event information, agenda, speakers, and sponsor profiles
A paid attendee registration and ticketing system with online payment processing
An exhibitor and sponsor management portal
An abstract and presentation submission system
A programme / session management system (agenda builder)
An attendee-facing mobile-responsive event app (web-based)
A live-event engagement module (Q&A, polls, networking)
An administrative back-end dashboard
Post-event resource repository and certificate generation
Out of scope: physical AV/streaming infrastructure, hotel booking, and travel logistics.

2. System Features & Functional Requirements
Module 1: Public Website & Content Management
FR-1.1 — Homepage & Branding
The homepage shall present a compelling, visually rich landing page including:
Event countdown timer (days, hours, minutes, seconds to 13 Aug 2026)
Hero flyer with symposium theme, date, venue, a hook that encourages them to register and primary CTA ('Register Now')
Sponsor/partner logo carousel (Platinum, Gold, Silver tiers with tier labeling)
Brief event summary aligned with the concept note narrative
Social media share buttons and links
FR-1.2 — About Section
Symposium background, rationale, and linkage to the 2nd NAS roadmap
Event objectives and expected outcomes (as per concept note)
Organizing committee profiles
Gallery for the previous symposiums 
PPT presentations of the second national agroecology symposium 
FR-1.3 — Programme / Agenda Page
Interactive two-day agenda (13–14 August) with session filters by sub-theme, type (plenary, panel, workshop), and time
Clickable session cards showing title, speakers, time, room, and description
'Add to personal schedule' button for registered attendees
Downloadable agenda (PDF export)
FR-1.4 — Speakers Page
Speaker profiles: photo, name, title, organization, country, bio, and assigned sessions
Search and filter by name, organization, or sub-theme
Keynote speakers prominently featured
FR-1.5 — Exhibitors & Sponsors Page
Tiered exhibitor showcase: Platinum, Gold, Silver
Each exhibitor: logo, description, website link, and downloadable materials
'Become a Sponsor' CTA linking to sponsorship application form
FR-1.6 — News & Blog
CMS-powered news/blog section for pre-event announcements, post-event summaries
Social sharing per article; RSS feed
FR-1.7 — Media & Press Kit
Downloadable press kit (concept note summary, logos, high-res images, official quote sheet)
Media accreditation form (separate from paid registration)
FR-1.8 — Contact & FAQ
Contact form with routing to organizing team email
Comprehensive FAQ addressing registration, payment, venue, accommodation, and logistics
FR-1.9 — CMS Administration
Admin-editable website content (all pages above) without developer involvement
Media library for logos, banners, and documents
SEO metadata management per page (title, description, OG tags)

FR-2.2 — Registration Form
The registration form shall collect:
Full name, professional title, organization, country, email, phone number
Attendee category selection (drives pricing)
Dietary requirements and accessibility needs
How they heard about the symposium (analytics)
Consent checkboxes: data privacy policy, photo/media consent, terms & conditions
Optional: LinkedIn profile URL, areas of interest (maps to sub-themes for networking)
Validation: real-time field validation with clear error messaging. Email uniqueness enforced per registration.
FR-2.3 — Group Registration
Organizations may register multiple attendees in a single session (batch registration)
Group discount tiers: 5–9 attendees (10% off), 10+ attendees (15% off) — configurable by admin
Single invoice generated for group bookings
FR-2.4 — Payment Processing
The payment module shall support:
MTN Mobile Money (Rwanda) — USSD push payment
Airtel Money (Rwanda) — USSD push payment
Visa / Mastercard (international and local cards)
Bank Transfer — generates proforma invoice; registration held pending manual confirmation by admin
Payment flow:
Attendee completes registration form
Directed to payment summary page (order review)
Selects payment method
Completes payment (real-time confirmation for MoMo/card)
Registration confirmed; automated email + SMS confirmation sent
E-ticket with QR code generated and attached to email
Failed or incomplete payments: system holds registration in 'pending' state for 24 hours before releasing the slot. Automated reminder email sent after 4 hours.
FR-2.5 — E-Ticket & QR Code
Each confirmed registration generates a unique QR-code-bearing e-ticket (PDF, mobile-friendly)
QR code encodes: attendee ID, name, category, and validity hash
Ticket emailed automatically; also accessible from attendee's account dashboard
FR-2.6 — Invoice & Receipt Management
Automatic receipt generation upon payment confirmation
Proforma invoice generation for bank transfer registrants
VAT handling configurable (on/off, percentage)
Admin can manually issue or override invoices
FR-2.7 — Cancellation & Refund Policy
Policy text displayed prominently during registration
Cancellation request form in attendee dashboard
Admin reviews and processes refunds manually; system logs all decisions
Transfer of registration to another attendee supported (admin-approved)
FR-2.8 — Waitlist Management
When a ticket category sells out, attendees can join a waitlist
Automated notification when a spot becomes available

Module 3: Attendee Dashboard & Self-Service Portal
FR-3.1 — Account Creation & Authentication
Registration creates an attendee account (email + password or OAuth via Google)
Email verification required before account activation
Password reset via email link
Optional two-factor authentication (2FA) via SMS or authenticator app
FR-3.2 — Attendee Dashboard Features
View and download e-ticket and receipt
Edit personal profile (name, title, org, photo, bio — for networking directory)
Personal agenda builder: add/remove sessions from the programme
Networking directory: browse and connect with other attendees (opt-in)
Notifications inbox: event updates, session reminders, announcements
Access to pre-event materials uploaded by organizers
Post-event: download presentation slides, access recordings, generate and download certificate of attendance

Module 4: Abstract & Presentation Submission
FR-4.1 — Submission Portal
Open submission form accessible to registered users and invited submitters
Fields: title, abstract (max 300 words, word counter), sub-theme selection, presentation type (oral, poster, workshop), author names and affiliations, keywords (up to 5)
File upload: supporting document (PDF/DOCX, max 10MB)
Submission deadline enforced with clear countdown; late submissions require admin override
FR-4.2 — Review Workflow
Submitted abstracts assigned to programme committee reviewers by admin
Reviewers score abstracts on configured criteria (relevance, quality, innovation)
Admin views consolidated scores and makes accept/reject/revision decision
Automated email notification to submitter with decision and feedback
Accepted presenters receive speaker portal access
FR-4.3 — Speaker Portal
Upload final presentation file (PDF/PPTX, max 50MB)
Confirm session slot and audio/visual requirements
View session details: date, time, room, chair/moderator
Speaker bio and photo management (feeds into public speakers page)

Module 5: Exhibitor & Sponsor Management
FR-5.1 — Sponsorship Application
Online application form: organization name, contact, desired tier, message
Admin reviews and approves; triggers payment invoice for sponsorship fee
Three tiers as per concept note: Platinum, Gold, Silver (benefits configured per tier)
FR-5.2 — Exhibitor Portal
Dedicated login for exhibitor contacts
Upload company logo, profile description, website URL, social links
Upload brochures and promotional materials (PDF, max 10MB each, up to 5 files)
Manage exhibitor staff passes (add/remove names within allocated quota)
View booth assignment details and event logistics information
Access lead capture tool: scan attendee QR codes to capture contact details (with attendee consent)
FR-5.3 — Exhibitor Analytics
Profile view count
Material download count
Leads captured (QR scans)

Module 6: Live Event Engagement
FR-6.1 — Event App (Mobile-Responsive Web)
Personalized agenda view with real-time session updates and room changes
Push notifications for session reminders (15 min before) and announcements
Interactive map of the venue (Kigali Marriott Hotel floor plan)
FR-6.2 — Session Engagement Tools
Live Q&A: attendees submit questions during sessions; moderator manages and displays approved questions
Live polls: presenter or admin launches polls; real-time results displayed on screen
Session rating: attendees rate sessions (1–5 stars + optional comment) after session ends
Chat/discussion thread per session (optional; admin-enabled)
FR-6.3 — Networking Module
Attendee directory with search and filter (by country, organization, sub-theme interest)
'Request to Connect' feature: attendee sends connection request; both parties get email notification
In-app messaging between connected attendees
AI-powered matchmaking suggestions based on shared interests and sub-themes (optional stretch goal)
FR-6.4 — QR Code Check-In
Dedicated check-in interface for registration desk staff (tablet/mobile browser)
Scan attendee QR code from e-ticket to mark attendance
Manual search check-in by name or registration ID for attendees without QR code
Real-time attendance dashboard for admin: arrivals by hour, by category

Module 7: Administration Dashboard
FR-7.1 — Registration Management
View, search, filter, and export all registrations (CSV/Excel)
Manually add, edit, or cancel registrations
Bulk import registrations via CSV template
Send targeted email/SMS to segments (all attendees, specific category, pending payment)
FR-7.2 — Financial Dashboard
Real-time revenue dashboard: total collected, by ticket category, by payment method
Transaction log with status (completed, pending, failed, refunded)
Daily, weekly, and custom-range revenue reports (PDF and CSV export)
Reconciliation report: registrations vs. confirmed payments
Manual payment confirmation for bank transfers
FR-7.3 — Content Management
Full CMS access to edit all public website content
Agenda builder: add/edit/delete sessions, assign speakers, assign rooms, set times
Speaker management: add/edit/approve speaker profiles
Announcement broadcaster: send notifications to all or segmented attendees
FR-7.4 — User & Role Management
RBAC with predefined roles: Super Admin, Finance Officer, Programme Committee, Registration Desk, Exhibitor Manager, Speaker
Custom role creation with granular permission settings
Audit log: all admin actions logged with timestamp and user ID
FR-7.5 — Analytics & Reporting
Registration conversion funnel (visits → started registration → completed payment)
Attendee demographics: by country, category, organization type
Session attendance heat map
Abstract submission and acceptance statistics
Exhibitor performance summary
Post-event satisfaction survey results



Module 8: Post-Event Module
FR-8.1 — Resource Repository
Admin uploads session recordings, presentation slides, and photos post-event
Organized by day, sub-theme, and session
Access control: public or registered-attendees-only per resource
FR-8.2 — Certificate of Attendance
Automated certificate generation for attendees who checked in (QR scanned)
Personalized: name, category, event name, dates, and organizer signature/logo
Downloadable as PDF from attendee dashboard
Admin can manually grant certificates to specific attendees
FR-8.3 — Post-Event Survey
Configurable survey sent automatically to all attendees 24 hours post-event
Question types: rating scale, multiple choice, open text
Results aggregated in admin dashboard with export

4.2 Security
HTTPS/TLS 1.2+ on all endpoints; HTTP → HTTPS redirect enforced
Passwords stored with bcrypt (minimum cost factor 12) — never in plain text
Payment card data never stored; all card processing via PCI-DSS compliant payment gateway (Flutterwave)
CSRF protection on all forms; XSS and SQL injection prevention (parameterised queries, output encoding)
Rate limiting on registration, login, and payment endpoints
Admin panel restricted by IP whitelist option
Regular automated vulnerability scanning (OWASP Top 10 baseline)
Data breach notification protocol documented and tested
4.3 Availability & Reliability
99.5% uptime SLA from 4 weeks before event through 1 week post-event
Automated database backups every 6 hours; point-in-time recovery
Graceful degradation: if payment gateway is unavailable, bank transfer option remains accessible
Health monitoring and alerting (e.g., Uptime Robot or AWS Cloud Watch) with < 2 min incident notification to admin
4.4 Scalability
Auto-scaling infrastructure to handle traffic spikes (e.g., when registration opens or a keynote speaker is announced)
CDN for static assets and media files
Database read replicas for reporting queries to avoid impacting transactional performance
4.5 Usability & Accessibility
WCAG 2.1 AA compliance: keyboard navigability, sufficient colour contrast, screen reader labels
Registration and payment flow completable on mobile in < 7 minutes
All critical user flows user-tested with at least 5 representative users before launch
Error messages specific and actionable (not generic 'error occurred' messages)
Support for right-to-left rendering (future-proofing)
4.6 Internationalisation & Localisation
Primary language: English
Secondary: Kinyarwanda UI labels and key content; French UI labels
Date formats: DD Month YYYY (e.g., 13 August 2026)
Currency: USD (primary display); RWF (secondary display with live rate)
Phone number validation supporting Rwanda (+250) and international formats
4.7 Maintainability
Codebase with clear module separation, documented APIs, and inline code comments
Admin CMS allowing non-technical staff to update all website content
Automated test coverage: minimum 70% unit/integration test coverage on backend
Staging environment mirroring production for pre-launch testing
Deployment pipeline (CI/CD) for zero-downtime deployments

8.2 Acceptance Criteria
The platform shall be accepted for launch when all of the following are verified:
All FR-1 through FR-8 functional requirements implemented and tested
Payment flow tested end-to-end with live gateway (at least 10 test transactions)
QR code check-in tested with 50 mock attendees
Load test passed: 500 concurrent users with < 2.5s page load
Security scan passed with no Critical or High OWASP vulnerabilities
Mobile usability test passed on iOS and Android devices
Admin trained and CMS content fully populated
DNS, SSL, email deliverability, and payment gateway all configured for production
